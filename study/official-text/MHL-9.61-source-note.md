# MHL § 9.61 official-source note

Retrieved: October 5, 2026. Exam cutoff under review: May 31, 2026.

## What the official Senate sources show

1. The official Article 9 table of contents lists **“9.61 Involuntary outpatient treatment.”** Source: https://legislation.nysenate.gov/pdf/laws/MHYTBA9
2. The direct official section endpoint, https://legislation.nysenate.gov/pdf/laws/MHY9.61, returns the whole Mental Hygiene Law table of contents instead of a § 9.61 section body.
3. The official § 9.63 PDF contains the current and future numbering for a different provision. Source: https://legislation.nysenate.gov/pdf/laws/MHY9.63

The PDF identifies **§ 9.63, Transportation of persons to or between hospitals**, as effective until June 30, 2027. It then prints the same provision under the future number **§ 9.61**, marked effective June 30, 2027:

```text
§ 9.61 Transportation of persons to or between hospitals.

In carrying out the transportation of any person to or between a
hospital, including a comprehensive psychiatric emergency program,
pursuant to the provisions of this article appropriate attempts shall be
made to elicit the cooperation of the person to be transported, prior to
resorting to compulsory means of transportation.

NB Effective June 30, 2027
```

## Result for the May 31, 2026 exam cutoff

The future transportation provision numbered § 9.61 was not yet effective on May 31, 2026. It is retained inside `mhl-sections/MHY9.63.pdf` as official cross-reference evidence and is not counted as a separate exam-cutoff section.

The future renumbering does not resolve the table-of-contents entry titled “Involuntary outpatient treatment.” Because the direct Senate endpoint supplies no section body for that entry, this collection does not claim or reproduce active May 31, 2026 text for it. The readable Article 9 collection therefore remains a 93-PDF collection with this single table-of-contents ambiguity documented rather than filled with inferred text.
