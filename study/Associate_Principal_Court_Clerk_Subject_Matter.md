# Subject of Examination: Associate Court Clerk (JG-24) and Principal Court Clerk (JG-26)

Official NY Courts subject lists, issued September 16, 2026. Transcribed from the official sheets by the owner on 2026-11-24 (the court site blocks automated downloads). The two lists are identical except item 5b, where Principal adds Social Services Law 384, 384-b and 384-c. `exam-scope.js` encodes these lists.

This will be a multiple-choice examination designed to assess knowledge of legal terminology, statutes, rules and procedures in effect in the New York State Unified Court System as of **September 30, 2026**, as set forth in:

1. Civil Practice Laws and Rules including but not limited to Articles 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12, 21, 22, 23, 30, 31, 32, 34, 41, 50, 51, 52, 54, 55, 63, 63-A, 70, and 78.
2. Criminal Procedure Law including but not limited to Articles 1, 10, 30, 100, 110, 120, 160, 170, 180, 190, 195, 200, 210, 216, 220, 260, 270, 280, 290, 300, 310, 320, 330, 340, 350, 360, 380, 390, 400, 410, 420, 430, 440 (sections 10-40, 46, 46a, and 47), 500, 510, 520, 530, 540, 550, 620, 690.05, 720, 722, 725, and 730.
3. Penal Law including but not limited to Articles 10, 55, 65, 70, 80, and 85.
4. Family Court Act including but not limited to Articles 1 (Parts 1, 5, 6, 7), 2 (Parts 4, 6), 3 (Parts 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8), 4, 5, 5-B (Parts 1, 2, 3, 4, 6), 6, 7, 8, 10, 10-A, 10-B, 10-C and 11 and any other statutes, rules and court procedures referred to therein;
5. Other laws and statutes including:
   - a. Domestic Relations Law Articles 3 (15-a), 5-A (Titles I, II, III), and 7 (Titles I, II, III), 10;
   - b. Social Services Law Sections 358-a and 383-c (Principal: 358-a, 383-c, 384, 384-b, and 384-c);
   - c. Uniform Rules for the New York State Trial Courts Parts 200, 202, 205 (Sections 2-5, 7b, 11, 12, 15, 17, 26, 28d, 29, 36, 37, 42-44, 49, 50, 52, 53, 59, 64, 67, 80, 86);
   - d. Corrections Law Articles 6C and 23;
   - e. Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law Article 7;
   - f. Vehicle and Traffic Law Sections 511, 1192, and 1193;
   - g. Mental Hygiene Law Articles 9, 10, and 81;
   - h. Executive Law Article 12-B (Section 259-I only) and Article 49-B.

NOTES: The passing score for the written examination will be determined at a date following the administration of the examination. In the First and Second Departments, all Court Clerks are Peace Officers and United States citizenship is required for candidates appointed in those departments.
