## Official source method

The controlling verification source was the New York State Legislature’s official [Laws of New York database](http://public.leginfo.state.ny.us/lawssrch.cgi?NVLWO:). At the time of review, its banner stated that the database was updated September 28, 2026 and current through 2026 Chapters 1–312. Complete section text was captured for all 58 unique provisions and checked on October 3, 2026.

The New York Senate’s official statute pages were also opened in the isolated `reference-audit` browser session. The Senate page for Penal Law § 55.05 loaded with its revision selector showing September 22, 2014. Cloudflare challenges later limited bulk review, so no blocked page was treated as verification evidence. The per-section Senate links below remain the public reading links stored in the dataset.

The `verified` field records the review date. It does not replace a source’s displayed revision date or the Legislature database’s currency date.

## Material corrections and qualifications

| Provision | Source-note problem | Verified treatment in the draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPL 30.10 | Presented subdivision 2 as a complete limitations table. | Labels the four periods as baselines and directs the reader to the offense-specific extensions, victim-age rules, public-servant rules, and tolling provisions. |
| CPL 30.30 | Used class A/class B misdemeanor shorthand and omitted the broader exception structure. | Uses the authorized-imprisonment split: over three months versus no more than three months; includes traffic infractions in the 30-day category; flags excluded offenses and excluded time. |
| CPL 100.10 | Said there were four local-court accusatory instruments. | Corrected to five and includes prosecutor’s information. |
| CPL 100.40 | Blended facial sufficiency with later replacement and disposition rules. | Limits the section summary to the instrument-specific facial-sufficiency tests and identifies later procedure as separately governed. |
| CPL 180.70 | Stated the class A reduction bar too broadly. | Notes the article 220 exception and the separate armed-felony bar. |
| CPL 195.10 | Needed confirmation of disqualifying charges and timing. | Retains the actual rule: held for grand-jury action, no class A felony punishable by death or life imprisonment, district-attorney consent, and waiver before indictment filing. No unsupported class B/C violent-felony bar was added. |
| CPL 200.20(5) | Described same-transaction consolidation as unconditionally mandatory. | Requires a defendant’s application and preserves the good-cause exception. |
| CPL 310.30 | Added an overbroad O’Rama “automatic reversal” statement without an official case-law source. | Uses the statutory notice, defendant-presence, return-to-court, and response rules only. Case-law gloss is excluded pending separate official decision review. |
| CPL 340.40 | Treated the misdemeanor jury rule as universal. | Adds the mandatory single-judge rule for a qualifying eligible youth with no prior crime conviction or youthful-offender adjudication and notes the mixed-charge rule. |
| CPL 390.20 | Described presentence reports as absolute requirements. | Adds current waiver routes and the New York City negotiated-sentence exception while preserving the baseline felony and listed-misdemeanor rules. |
| CPL 510.10 | Used the former “least restrictive” formulation. | Uses the current text: individualized flight-risk determination, the kind and degree of control or restriction necessary to reasonably assure return, a consistent securing order, and an explanation on the record or in writing. |
| CPL 520.40 | Called cash-bail transfer automatic. | Corrected to a request by the accused, notice to the depositor, and a superior-court transfer order, with overage and deficiency rules. |
| CPL 530.40 | Overgeneralized superior-court bail authority. | Separates below-felony-only charges from felony charges and applies the qualifying-offense limits. |
| CPL 720.20 | Relied on case-law framing even though the current statute states the duty. | Grounds the mandatory eligible-youth determination and replacement of conviction directly in the current statute. |
| PL 55.05 | Said misdemeanors had only classes A and B. | Corrected to three categories: class A, class B, and unclassified. |
| PL 55.10 | Treated every outside-Penal-Law unspecified misdemeanor as class A. | Distinguishes a misdemeanor designation with no class or sentence (class A) from a sentence-defined misdemeanor (generally unclassified) and preserves subdivision 3 exceptions. |
| PL 80.00 / 80.05 | Presented dollar figures without their statutory alternatives and exceptions. | Marks the figures as general ceilings and flags gain-based, listed-offense, unclassified-misdemeanor, environmental, and other statutory alternatives. |

## Editorial decisions

- All summaries are paraphrases. No statutory quotation is presented as the summary.
- “Official sample question” labels were removed because no official released sample was independently reviewed in this audit.
- Unsupported exam-frequency claims were removed. Tags describe subject matter or an identified correction only.
- The draft module remains inactive. Its generated question banks and presentation choices are unapproved working material and were not treated as verified product content.
