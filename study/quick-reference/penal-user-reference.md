# Penal Law Quick Reference — Court Clerk (55-858) / Senior Court Clerk (55-859)

6 entries across 3 articles. User-supplied notes; verify before publication.

## Penal Law Article 10 — Definitions
- **PL 10.00(1)-(6)** — Offense means conduct with a possible imprisonment or fine sentence under state or local law. Violation: 15 days or less. Misdemeanor: over 15 days but not over 1 year. Felony: imprisonment over 1 year is possible. Crime: misdemeanor or felony only, not a violation or traffic infraction.
- **PL 10.00(7)** — Person means a human being and, where appropriate, a public or private corporation, unincorporated association, partnership, government, or governmental instrumentality.

## Penal Law Article 55 — Classification & Designation of Offenses
- **PL 55.05** — Felony classes: A-I, A-II, B, C, D, E. Misdemeanor classes: A or B; no third class.
- **PL 55.10** — Outside the Penal Law, an unspecified felony is Class E and an unspecified misdemeanor is Class A. A traffic infraction is never deemed a violation or misdemeanor regardless of sentence.

## Penal Law Article 80 — Fines
- **PL 80.00** — Baseline felony maximum fine: higher of $5,000 or double gain, triple gain for Article 496. Certain Article 220/221 drug felony maxima: A-I $100,000; A-II $50,000; B $30,000; C $15,000.
- **PL 80.05** — Class A misdemeanor maximum $1,000; Class B $500; violation $250.
